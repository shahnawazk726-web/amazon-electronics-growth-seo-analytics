
# 🚀 Amazon Electronics Growth & SEO Analytics Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This project simulates a real-world **US Amazon Electronics & Gadgets** e-commerce business over a 24-month period, analyzing how SEO performance, content strategy, and marketing spend directly influence revenue growth and customer acquisition cost (CAC). The analysis demonstrates measurable, data-driven strategies to optimize marketing ROI.

## 💡 Core Skills Demonstrated
- SEO Strategy & Keyword Rank Analysis
- Growth Marketing & Customer Acquisition
- Data Analytics & Visualization
- Content Marketing Performance Tracking
- Business Intelligence Dashboard Design

## 🏗️ Data Architecture & Pipeline
- Synthetic dataset generated using Python (Pandas/NumPy) simulating 12,000+ e-commerce transactions
- 24-month timeline (Sep 2024 – Aug 2026) with realistic seasonal trends (Q4 Black Friday surge)
- Key fields: Date, Traffic Source, Organic Keyword Rank, Sessions, Clicks, Conversions, Revenue, Ad Spend, Content Type

## 📈 Deep-Dive Insights

1. **SEO-Driven Traffic Delivers Superior Conversion Efficiency** — Organic search traffic converted more efficiently than paid channels, especially when keywords ranked in the top 10-20 positions.
2. **Strong Inverse Correlation Between Keyword Rank & Revenue** — As organic keyword rankings improved, revenue per session increased significantly, proving SEO's compounding ROI.
3. **Q4 Seasonal Surge Amplified by Content Engagement** — November-December showed sharp revenue spikes correlated with high engagement on Product and Landing pages.
4. **Underperforming Paid Channels Diluting Marketing Efficiency** — Certain paid campaigns showed high spend but low conversion efficiency compared to SEO.

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Reallocate 20-30% of underperforming ad spend** into SEO content production to reduce long-term CAC.
2. **Prioritize top-of-SERP keyword targeting** for high-margin categories (Laptops, Cameras).
3. **Build a pre-Q4 SEO content calendar** 60-90 days in advance to capture organic holiday traffic.
